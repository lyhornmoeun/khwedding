Sacred Rituals & Etiquette

Honor Tradition Without Overspending

Cambodian parents value authenticity and merit (បុណ្យកុសល) far more than lavish ballroom rentals. Here is how we preserve each sacred rite gracefully.

១

Pithi Hae Neak Ta (ជំនូនផ្លែឈើ)

ពិធីហែជំនូន និងផ្លែឈើ ៣៦ មុខ

The groom’s morning procession bringing seasonal fruits. Instead of imported expensive fruits, we source sweet Siem Reap mangoes, Battambang oranges, and Kampot bananas presented in handwoven artisan rattan trays.

Saves ~$350 in packaging & import costs
២

Pithi Kat Sok (កាត់សក់បង្កក់សិរី)

ពិធីកាត់សក់សម្អាតស្នេហ៍

The symbolic hair-cutting rite representing purification and cleansing past karma. Our experienced Achars craft a joyful, witty performance that delights elderly relatives while maintaining deep spiritual sanctity.

Includes traditional silver mirrors & perfumed water
៣

Sompeah Ptem (សំពះផ្ទឹម)

ពិធីចងដៃ និងបង្វិលពពិល

The red thread tying and passing of candle blessings. Held beneath our open-air wooden gazebo in Siem Reap, this sacred rite connects generations without requiring an expensive air-conditioned hotel ballroom.

100% natural sacred red string & blessed areca flowers
